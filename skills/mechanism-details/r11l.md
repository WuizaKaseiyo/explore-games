# r11l — tethered-throw-placement

## Summary
The player has a single click action (ACTION6) on a 16×16 cell grid. Each
click selects a "leg piece" (`bdkazLeg-*`) from the tray and animates it
toward the click target. As it travels, attached "head" pieces (`bdkaz-*`)
follow rigidly, and the leg auto-snaps the head to the centroid of all leg
pieces in its group (`bdahxidrjf`). Some heads have lock targets (`kzeze-*`)
they need to land on; landing on a lock with matching colour-set "winning"s
the group; landing on an obstacle (`qtwnv*`) shakes and counts a fail. After
5 fails, lose. Step budget 60.

## Action mapping

| Action | Semantic (game-specific) | Gate / when valid |
|---|---|---|
| ACTION6 | Click — either pick a tray leg, or click a target square to throw the selected leg there | always; the system internally distinguishes pick vs throw by what was clicked |

(`available_actions=[6]`. The valid-action list is a fixed 16×16 grid of
ACTION6 ActionInputs at multiples of 4 — `for x,y in 0..64 step 4`.)

## Per-level mechanic progression

| Level | Mechanic introduced (or composed) | Specific challenge / constraint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Single leg, single head, single lock | tutorial: click leg, click target on the lock |
| 2 | Multiple legs but they all share one head (centroid follows leg group) | placing the leg shifts the head's centre |
| 3 | Multiple groups (head1+legs1, head2+legs2 ...); each must reach its own lock | parallel completion |
| 4 | Decorative obstacle (`qtwnv*`) — landing the leg on it triggers shake animation; 5 strikes = lose | fail counter `anzbz` |
| 5 | Per-group "anqcf" annotation — those heads do not need locks (they just need to not collide) | special-case in win check |
| 6 | Composite: many groups, obstacles, mixed pickup/move/winning conditions | full stack |

## Win condition
After throw animation completes, every group's head must collide with its
lock (or be of "anqcf" type which just needs no collision-fail). When
`ilynklqzjc` holds → `winning=True`, after one more step where no
"miyy"-pulsing remains, `self.next_level()`.

## Lose condition
- 5 obstacle strikes (`anzbz >= 5`) → `self.lose()`.
- Step budget exhausted (`_action_count >= _max_actions = 60`) →
  `self.lose()`.

## Internal state
- selected leg (obfuscated `mjdkn`); `tfhng` is its index
- group registry `brdck` — dict per group of `kignw` (head), `mdpcc` (legs),
  `xwdrv` (lock)
- per-group lock-target state `dfugm` (animation of "lockon" pulses)
- `bmtib` flag — animation in progress; `tjffy`/`gfwuu` interpolate position
- pickup-paint list `xigcb-*` — "skin" sprites that, on collision with head,
  paint pixels onto the head sprite
- shake animation `cyaxdmynov`/`zxankdpxcx`/`wvopxkipus` for obstacle hits
- step counter HUD `_step_counter_ui` (`bdxsqgndfy`)

## Notable code patterns
- Centroid-follow for tethered pieces: `bdahxidrjf` averages leg positions to
  recompute head position. Reusable as "rigid group with centroid anchor".
- Pre-enumerated 256-entry valid-action grid: the agent always has the same
  256 ACTION6 candidates at `(x*4, y*4)`. Lets the game treat clicks as
  cell-quantised.
- Pickup-paint mechanic: a sprite passing through a `xigcb-*` "paint" sprite
  copies its non-(-1) pixels onto the head, and the paint is consumed.
- Obstacle shake: `mpjrv-bdkaz` ghost sprite blinks on/off while a counter
  ramps to 5; visual feedback for fail count.
- Multi-pulse animation per group via dict-of-dicts state — each group has
  its own animation timer, all advance together in `dkhjddmkke`.
