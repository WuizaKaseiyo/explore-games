## Mechanic

The board hosts a small population of "polarity pawns" — coloured square sprites whose interior pattern encodes one of two binary states (yang shows a yellow top half; yin shows a magenta bottom half). The player has only two verbs: clicking on a pawn (ACTION6) flips its polarity yang↔yin, and pressing the freedom slot (ACTION5) advances one global "attract tick" in which every pawn takes one cell step toward its nearest same-colour opposite-polarity peer. Same-colour yang+yin pairs that end up adjacent (Chebyshev distance ≤ pawn size) discharge — both removed. Win = empty board. Levels compose: L1 establishes flip + attract + discharge; L2 adds colour-keying (only same-colour pawns attract and discharge, so the player must flip the right pawns to set up valid pairs); L3 adds auto-flip-pads at wall-gaps so the player must plan polarity around a forced mid-walk pad-flip and re-flip the post-pad pawn at the right moment.
