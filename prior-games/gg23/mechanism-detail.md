# gg23 - braid-strand-weave

Textile topology puzzle. `ACTION6` on a crossing cycles it through straight,
left-over, and right-over. Active crossings swap adjacent strand lanes, so the
bottom tapestry depends on braid topology rather than wire routing.

Level 1 teaches pure braid structure: crossings have small lock stitches that
show the required over/under state, and the bottom target plates show the final
strand order. Level 2 adds dye transfer: at each active crossing the over-strand
bleeds its colour into the under-strand, so final target colours depend on both
lane topology and over/under choice. Level 3 adds tension: active crossings
stress both participating strands, and one over-stressed strand must be smoothed
by clicking an ordinary strand segment before the final weave can be accepted.

The visual register is deliberately unlike the earlier block grids: thick
ribbons, highlights, shadows, woven background threads, lock stitches, bottom
tapestry targets, and tension pips use pixel-level detail across the 64x64
frame.
