## Plain-English mechanic summary

The avatar walks a chain of floor islands separated by pits that are bridged by coloured platforms hanging from overhead pulley wheels. Clicking a pulley wheel selects it; the per-game freedom action toggles the selected pulley, instantly inverting both of its platforms (the LOW one rises, the HIGH one falls). On L2 a small carryable peg, picked up by walking onto it and dropped by walking onto a matching socket, removes a wall blocking the goal corridor. On L3 two of the pulleys are connected by an overhead cable that links their states in opposite phase, so a single toggle action acts on both at once.
