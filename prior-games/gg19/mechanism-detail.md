# gg19 — marble-drop-switchyard

Side-view marble-run based on the revised `Marble-Drop-Switchyard` idea.
`ACTION6` launches tray marbles, flips Y-switches, places limited friction
rugs, or triggers a one-shot track lever. `ACTION5` advances marbles along
diagonal ramp nodes toward cups.

Level 1 is cup routing with live switches. Level 2 adds speed and friction:
one route must be slowed before a critical switch. Level 3 uses a lever and
mixer cups so same-colour pairs fuse into derived colours before scoring.
The visual layer now uses thin rail strokes and small ramp nodes instead of
full block tiles, so clickable switches are visually distinct from ordinary
track. Switch tiles render the active outgoing arm and coloured mid-edge route
pips. Level 2 adds a black control wire between the switch nodes, while level
3 adds a gold wire from the lever into the switchyard; before activation it is
dotted, and after activation it becomes a continuous gold bypass showing that
only the gold marble is allowed through that route. A harmonised right-side
target panel shows the required output colours for every level and fills rows
as marbles score.
